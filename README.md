# Hi, I'm Jadheer Ahsan K.P.

**Research Assistant** at Qatar University College of Medicine, Doha, Qatar

I work on computational approaches to cancer genomics, with a focus on somatic variant classification, pathway analysis, and survival modelling in breast cancer. My current work combines whole-exome sequencing data with machine learning to identify and classify pathogenic mutations in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC).

I am preparing for a PhD in Genomics and Precision Medicine (computational track, Fall 2027), with interests in computational oncology, variant interpretation, and AI-driven biomarker discovery.

## Current Projects

**Somatic Mutations in TNBC** -- ML-based pathogenicity classification of somatic variants using WES data (PRJEB32259, 19 patients). Built a logistic regression classifier (CADD + REVEL) that outperformed CADD alone (AUC 0.971 vs 0.963, internal; 0.994 vs 0.987, external validation on 254 FUSCC patients). Includes driver gene analysis, pathway enrichment, and survival modelling in METABRIC (n=299) and TCGA cohorts. Manuscript in preparation.

**EGFR T790M Molecular Docking** -- Structure-based drug design pipeline for EGFR resistance mutations. Full workflow: ligand preparation (Avogadro), docking (PyRx/AutoDock Vina), ADMET profiling, and molecular dynamics simulation (GROMACS).

**SGLT-2 Inhibitor Metabolite Profiling** -- Collaborative project on anti-doping research (QADA Grant, Project 1164). HepG2 cell-based metabolite identification for SGLT-2 inhibitors.
